# Velessoft / Open Workshop

An open-source workshop: crisp tool surfaces, editorial labels and the lime/violet contrast. The identity must remain visible in an ordinary form or table, without a decorative hero.

## Visual grammar

- Graphite or paper is the main surface. Avoid ambient gradient blobs and glowing backgrounds.
- Controls and panels use 2px corners. Separate regions with visible rules; do not wrap every group in a floating card.
- Lime identifies the primary action. Its 2px violet offset shadow is a small, repeatable signature. Reserve larger hard shadows for editorial callouts.
- Violet marks selection, focus and structural accents. Status colors retain their own meaning and accompanying text.
- Use proportional text for reading and labels. Monospace belongs to metadata, counters, code and short section labels. Do not turn long paragraphs into terminal text.
- Short slash marks and ruled headings supply identity. Avoid random stickers, fake terminal logs, decorative warning tape and rotation in working screens.

## Usability is part of the style

Readable 14px body text, 36px desktop controls and 44px coarse-pointer targets. Preserve native keyboard behavior, visible focus, contrast and descriptive labels. Density comes from layout, not tiny hit targets.

## Glass and motion

Glass is an optional thin overlay for the header and dialogs, not the visual identity of every surface. Keep a crisp edge; avoid broad soft shadows. Tables and forms remain solid. Transitions are short and local, never perpetual. Respect reduced motion/transparency and provide explicit off switches.

## Scope

Shared CSS is framework-independent. Native React behavior must not be advertised as equivalent Vue/Svelte wrappers until those adapters have been built and tested.
